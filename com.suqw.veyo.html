Privacy Policy & Consent

Your Privacy Comes First

This page explains, in plain language, what information handles, why it is handled, and the choices you have. We keep the design simple and the wording clear so you can make an informed decision.

We do not use your data for analytics, profiling, or advertising, and we do not sell it or share it with third parties.

Information We Handle

  • App settings and preferences you choose, such as language, theme, and display options. These stay on your device.
  • Anonymous device signals used only to confirm that the app runs correctly on your hardware and operating system version.
  • Diagnostic information produced locally when something fails, used solely to help you or our support team resolve the issue.

We do not collect names, addresses, phone numbers, contact lists, precise location history, browsing history, or any other content that could identify you personally.

How Information Is Used

  • To remember your preferences between sessions so the app feels consistent.
  • To keep the app stable and compatible with your device.
  • To respond when you contact support with a question or a problem.

Each purpose is limited to what is strictly necessary. We never repurpose your information for analytics, marketing, or any secondary use.

No Analytics, No Third Parties

We do not embed analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking pixels. No data is transmitted to third-party services for analysis, profiling, or resale.

  • No behavioral or usage analytics.
  • No advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking.
  • No data sharing, licensing, or selling to third parties.

Storage and Security

Information that is kept on your device is protected by the operating system's own sandboxing and encryption. We apply reasonable technical measures to keep any information we handle safe, and we retain it only as long as necessary for the purposes described above.

When information is no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymized in accordance with our internal retention practices.

Your Choices and Rights

  • Review or change app settings at any time within the app.
  • Clear locally stored preferences by removing the app or using your device settings.
  • Contact us to ask questions about this policy or to request deletion of any information you have sent us.

You can withdraw consent at any time by discontinuing use of the app or by uninstalling it. Doing so will not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Updates to This Policy

If we ever update this policy, the revised version will be presented in the app before it takes effect, so you can review the changes and decide whether to continue. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact Us

Questions about privacy or this policy? Reach out and we will respond as soon as possible.

By tapping “Agree & Continue” you confirm that you have read and understood this policy.
